## Videos
Any story slot can be a video instead. Name it `.mp4` and add its key to the `VIDEOS` list in `index.html`:

```js
const VIDEOS = ['dancing-2','everyone-1'];
```

They play muted and silent, the way they do on Instagram.

## Sizes
Circles and portraits are shown small — 800px wide is plenty. Story photographs fill the screen; 1600px on the long edge is more than enough. Keep each under about 500KB so the page opens quickly on a phone at the party.
