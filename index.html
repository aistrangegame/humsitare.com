THE FESTIVAL

What is Diwali?

Diwali comes from Deepavali, a row of lamps. It is marked across India and around the world, on the new-moon night of autumn — the darkest night of the month.

That is the whole festival in one picture: a row of lamps against the darkest night.

It runs five days, each with its own reason to light a lamp. Tap one.

Lakshmi Puja — The main night. Lamps everywhere, and Lakshmi, goddess of prosperity and well-being, is welcomed into every home.

Customs differ by region and by family. This is one common telling.

THE WAY ARCHANA TELLS IT

This season holds the memory of three ages.

Satyug

THE CHURNING · IT REVERSES

Lakshmi rises from the churning of the cosmic ocean and is welcomed as abundance itself. It is why we light the way to our doors.

Treta

THE RETURN · IT COMPLETES

After fourteen years of exile and the defeat of Ravana, Ram returns to Ayodhya. The people light rows of lamps to guide him home.

Dwapar

THE SHELTER · IT HOLDS

Krishna lifts Govardhan hill to shelter the people of Vraj from Indra’s storm. Annakut, a mountain of food, is offered in its memory.

Amavasya · the night with no moon

AND NOW

Lit far from Ayodhya.

For families living away from where it began, the meaning has grown. It is not only about remembering a homecoming. It is how we make one.

Archana adds one more light for our own time: Saraswati, goddess of knowledge, music and the arts, who arrives every year when the stage lights up.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few things, before the night

Where do we park?

Right in front of the building, at 1370 Adams Road. There is room for everyone.

What time should we actually arrive?

Any time from 5. The puja begins shortly after, so come before it if you would like to be there for it.

What should we wear?

Festive Indian. Nobody has ever been underdressed or overdressed at one of these.

Do we need to bring anything?

Only yourselves. If you would like to bring something sweet, made rather than bought is the tradition here.

Are children welcome?

They are half the point. Bring them, and put their names on the reply so we count them in.

Do we have to perform?

No. Every family is invited to, and most do, but nobody is ever made to. Tell us your song if you want one.

What if our plans change?

Come back to the reply, find it with your phone number, and change it there. Or just message us.
