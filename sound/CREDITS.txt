RECORDED
  bell.mp3     A ghanta (Hindu ritual bell) struck with a leather mallet.
               "274316 the-very-real-horst ghanta-leather-mallet-2015-05-16.wav"
               by the_very_Real_Horst, Wikimedia Commons — CC0 (public domain dedication).
               Made calm for this page: one strike taken an octave lower, its bright
               partials filtered away, a slow one-second bloom instead of a strike,
               a soft singing-bowl tone beneath it tuned to the same Sa, and a long
               tail in a large quiet space. Rings for about thirteen seconds.

MADE FOR THIS PAGE
  tanpura.mp3  A tanpura drone, Pa–Sa–Sa–low Sa, plucked strings with the jawari buzz
               of the bridge. Tuned so Sa sits exactly three octaves below the bell.
               An 8.8-second loop. No freely licensed pure tanpura recording exists on
               Commons, so this one was synthesised.
  swell.mp3    The swell when the page lights: Sa, Pa and high Sa opening slowly.
  hum.mp3      The low breathing hum under the dark lamp. A loop.
  launch.mp3   A rocket leaving the ground: a soft rising whoosh.
