## 1 · Inhabit

**Mechanism line:** one night that becomes morning across fifteen feet, carried by a single flame passed lamp to lamp along one thread; five forgettings and five rememberings live inside that one night; read whole from across the room, opened up close, finished in the hand.
**Room test — nearest thing it is not:** a mural of five framed paintings with captions (or a museum timeline panel). "One night", "one flame passed" and "finished in the hand" keep it from that: the scenes are not separate, the light is continuous, and the words live on the phone, not the wall.
**Readers:** a guest at ~20–30 ft (from the floor / dance area), a guest at 2–3 ft (in front of the wall), a guest in the hand (phone after a scan). Also Arch, who reads it first.

## 2 · Blink

**Gaia — who arrives, in what state?** (a) guests walking in at 5 pm, coats on, the wall is the first thing they pass; (b) guests drifting back between dances, a drink in hand, curious; (c) children at 3–4 ft eye level hunting for hidden codes. → candidate: the bottom band is the children's band (meets Lalita).
**Neev — what does it stand on?** (a) the night sky as the ground, deep indigo to dawn cream; (b) the earth/ground line running all 15 ft (Bhudevi's ground in story 1 becomes Ayodhya's road in 5); (c) the fabric itself as the ground: the weave shows through in the light end. → (b) meets Sakshi: the ground is the road home.
**Shweta — what is the medium?** (a) fabric under warm string light and sweeping coloured moving heads; (b) the far view needs big value contrast and few shapes; the near view needs detail that rewards 2 ft; (c) dark ink on fabric drinks light, so the dark end needs a sheen or gold line to read at all. → (c) meets Bindu: gold line.
**Sid — what holds it up unseen?** (a) one golden thread running the full 15 ft, stitching every lamp: the structural line of the whole composition; (b) a strict three-band grid: lamps on top, scenes in the middle, ground and codes at the bottom; (c) a seamless print or a seam hidden at a story boundary. → (a) meets Bandi Chhor's 52 threads and Narakasura's "same thread".
**Arch — how does it speak?** (a) one quiet title per story in small caps under its scene, nothing else; (b) no words at all on the wall, only the opening line at the far left; (c) the opening line at the left, closing line at the right, titles small. → (c) frames the walk like a sentence.
**Bindu — what moves?** (a) the flame: it travels left to right, lamp by lamp; (b) the light level: the whole field brightens; (c) the hall's moving lights sweep across it, so dark-end gold flickers like real flame. → (c) the room animates a still print.
**Sakshi — what is revealed only by travelling?** (a) walking left to right, you age from night to morning; (b) hidden codes seen only up close; (c) from far you see a gradient; up close the gradient is made of many tiny lamps and figures. → (c) the dark is made of unlit lamps.
**Karishma — what works before anyone looks?** (a) from the doorway it reads as "dark to light" in one glance; (b) no face needs to be read for the story to land; (c) the final 3 ft glows even under dim light (cream ground, bright gold).
**Ashrey — what is it, as one body?** (a) a single night; (b) a thread being sewn; (c) a road home. → meeting: **a golden thread sewn through one night, which becomes the road home.**
**Lalita — the one unexplained thing?** (a) one lamp in the dark end is already lit, tiny, with no story attached; (b) a 53rd tassel on the cloak, for the guest; (c) the last lamp has no clay around it, just light, as if it were the viewer. → (b) quietly invites the guest into Bandi Chhor's cloak.

### Meetings (where Converge begins)
1. **The golden thread** (Sid × Shweta × Ashrey × Bandi Chhor): one gold line runs the full 15 ft. It carries the lamps, it becomes the 52 cloak threads in story 4, and it becomes Ayodhya's road in story 5. Fabric house, thread stories, and a gold line that reads on dark cloth.
2. **The dark is made of unlit lamps** (Sakshi × Shweta): from far, a gradient; up close, every dark patch is clay lamps waiting.
3. **The room is the flame** (Bindu × Shweta): gold ink plus the moving lights gives flicker without a single moving part.
4. **Children's band** (Gaia × Lalita): bottom band at 4–5 ft holds the main codes and a few hidden ones at a child's reach on tiptoe.
